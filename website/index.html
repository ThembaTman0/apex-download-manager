Features

Everything a download deserves

Built around a multi-connection Rust engine, wrapped in an interface that stays out of your way.

⇉

Parallel connections

Up to 32 simultaneous streams per file saturate your bandwidth — with automatic retry on stalls and drops.

⏸

True pause & resume

Per-segment progress is saved continuously. Pause, reboot, come back tomorrow — it picks up exactly where it left off.

⌁

Browser capture

A lightweight extension hands downloads to Apex automatically, with a right-click “Download with Apex” on any link.

◷

Scheduling & queue

Start downloads at a chosen time, cap concurrency and speed, and let it sleep or shut down your PC when it finishes.

✓

Checksum verification

Paste the publisher’s SHA-256 and get a definitive match — know the file is authentic before you run it.

⌘

Keyboard-first

New download, search, pause, resume, delete — all without touching the mouse. A clipboard watcher catches copied links.

Safety

Downloads you can trust

Most download managers bypass the safety net your browser provides. Apex rebuilds it — and adds its own.

  • Files are tagged for Windows SmartScreen & Defender scanning, just like browser downloads
  • Resume integrity checks prevent silently corrupted files when a server’s file changes
  • The browser extension requests zero website permissions — it cannot read your pages
  • Insecure (HTTP) sources are clearly flagged
  • 100% local: no account, no cloud, no telemetry
